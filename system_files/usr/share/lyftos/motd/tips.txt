Need an application? Open Bazaar to browse, install, and update Flatpak apps.
Use `ujust --choose` to browse the setup tools inherited from Bazzite.
Use `bootc status` to check your booted image and any staged or rollback deployment.
An OS rollback does not restore your home directory, Flatpak apps, Homebrew packages, or Distrobox containers. Keep backups of your files.
Prefer command-line tools? Run `brew help` to explore Homebrew.
Want a quieter terminal? Run `ujust toggle-user-motd` to hide or restore this welcome.
Update break something? Inspect the previous deployment before rolling back. [Read the lyftOS recovery guide](https://github.com/dipilo/lyftos-image/blob/main/README.md#getting-back).
